
🌊 DPWH Flood Control Analysis (2018–2025)
An Investigation into Budget Allocation vs. Project Completion: A Case of Ghost Projects and Fiscal Gaps.

Can a project be funded but never exist? In my latest analysis of the DPWH Flood Control Program, the data reveals a startling "Implementation-Spending Gap" where ₱162B is tied up in projects that haven't even broken ground.

📊 Executive Summary
An analysis of Department of Public Works and Highways (DPWH) data from 2018 to 2025 reveals a significant disparity between infrastructure funding and actual execution. Despite ₱705B in allocations for over 11,000 projects, billions are tied up in "not yet started" and "ghost" projects, particularly in major urban centers

🚩 Critical Findings 

1. The Fiscal Gap
   
Metric                                                 	Figure
Total Allocated Budget	                                ₱705 Billion
Total Spent to Date	                                    ₱525 Billion
Completion Rate                                       	73.49% (9,459 Projects)
"Not Yet Started" Projects                            	1,990 Projects
Sunk Cost (Non-started)	                                ₱162 Billion

2. City-Level Execution Benchmarks

Manila and Davao demonstrate strong project delivery, while Quezon City and Caloocan show alarming levels of non-implementation.
•	🏆 Davao City: 99.28% Completion (Top Performer).
•	✅ Manila: 89.2% Completion.
•	⚠️ Caloocan: 74.44% Completion.
•	❌ Quezon City: Most projects received (225 projects / ₱10B) but highest number of non-started and "ghost" projects (34 recorded by Philippine Star; 56 in current data).

🔍 Concentration of Risk

The 2022 "Spending Peak"

In 2022—a year of slow post-pandemic recovery—flood control projects peaked at 4,000 projects worth ₱187B. This surge in spending coincided with a period of lower-than-average execution speed.
Contractor Monopoly & "Ghost" Projects
A significant portion of funding is concentrated among a few select firms. Notably, firms linked to "ghost projects" continue to receive high-value contracts.
•	Top Recipients: Sunwest Inc., Legacy Construction, QM Builders, Alpha & Omega (Discaya), and Royal Crown Monarch.

💡 Recommendations for Policy & Oversight
1.	Audit of "Not Yet Started" Funds: Investigation into the ₱162B allocated to projects that have seen zero progress.
2.	Contractor Blacklisting: Stricter vetting for firms linked to "ghost projects" or repeated non-completion.
3.	Regional Decentralization: Shift focus from Quezon City-centric funding to high-risk provinces with better execution track records.
